**Date:** 2026-07-20 · **Status:** design for author review, pre-plan ·
**Sub-project A of a four-workstream program** (Understand · Share · Run · Use) decomposed
in-session 2026-07-20. B–D get their own spec→plan cycles later; this spec builds A only.

## What this is

The 2026-07-19/20 sitting produced a large, coherent doctrinal arc in dialogue — the
UoD/commens distinction, institution-can't-happen-in-an-individual, the three EPG roles as a
*model* of the institution, the S/A parameterization and its West correspondence, the refusal
of "final," and the standing statement that the higher-order frontier is deliberately not
crossed. **That doctrine currently exists only in the conversation and is at risk.** Workstream
A captures it and writes the program down, so B/C/D have a conforming foundation to build on.

Two deliverables plus a minimal ripple set (Approach 1, author-approved 2026-07-20):

1. **A new doctrine doc** — `docs/THE_COMMENS_AND_THE_COMMUNITY.md`.
2. **A rewritten `docs/ROADMAP.md`** — the program doc, in place, organized by the four
   workstreams, superseding the 2026-06-27 consolidation.
3. **A minimal ripple set** — two GLOSSARY entries + three one-paragraph/clause pointers, so
   the new vocabulary isn't stranded. All wider harmonization is **routed to sweep B**, not done
   here.

**Explicit non-goals for A** (routed to B): Quarto-book membership of the new doc; harmonizing
older docs (README, CAPABILITY_MAP, VISION_AND_SCOPE, the membranes docs) to the new
vocabulary; the publication first-paper choice (carried in ROADMAP as an author decision, not
made here). A is writing + program-capture, not the sweep.

## The ratified spine vs. the flagged glosses

The doctrine doc must keep two registers visibly distinct, using the house pattern already used
elsewhere ("assistant's reading, flagged"):

- **[ratified]** — the author stated or corrected it directly this sitting. Written as doctrine.
- **[flagged]** — an assistant elaboration the author engaged with but has not ruled on. Carried
  in place, marked, and gathered into the closing **Open verdicts** list.

Where a section has a ratified spine and a flagged detail, both tags appear and the flagged
sentences are marked in situ.

## Deliverable 1 — `docs/THE_COMMENS_AND_THE_COMMUNITY.md`

House-style title, matching THE_KYTOS / THE_MEASURE_OF_KNOWLEDGE. **Not** added to
`docs/_quarto.yml` in A (book membership is B's decision).

**Epigraph:** Job 42:5–6 in Robert Alter's translation ("By the hearing of the ear I heard
You… Therefore do I recant, and I repent in dust and ashes"), the seal the author gave the
arc. *(Author confirmed 2026-07-20: include.)*

**Section list** (order tracks the arc as it was built; every section carries its tag):

1. **The pair: UoD and commens** *[ratified]* — UoD = the immediately
   accessible / controllable / attested internal model *inside* the membrane, what a kytos
   thinks *with*. Commens = the between / outside / before / after that makes communication
   possible without being possessed — interacted-with, never internalized, **not an Arisbe
   structure**; a **regulative** concept, not to be operationalized. The explicit category-mistake
   warning: the attested corpus is the *internalized UoD*, **not** the commens (correcting the
   in-session error). **The commens is a social construct** (author, 2026-07-20): real-for-participants
   (it confronts them with facticity and exceeds any one of them) *and* **sustained only by
   participation** — Berger/Luckmann's objectivated reality, not a pre-given or timeless one:
   *if we do not participate, it disappears.* Its "before us and after us" is the persistence of a
   continuously reproduced activity across finite participants, not the standing of an eternal
   thing — **open, and therefore precarious.** Import = internalizing a portion of the outside;
   export = contributing to the outside, which then becomes part of others' potential commens
   (and helps sustain it).
2. **What the pair grounds** *[flagged]* — (a) the measure is a vector, never a scalar over
   agents, *because* no commens-scaled denominator exists to normalize across agents (the
   un-possessability of the commens is the ground, not a policy choice); (b) the
   correspondence-not-truth floor is the instance's acknowledgment that its UoD is a finite
   internalization facing a commens it does not own.
3. **The institution and the sub-roles** *[ratified spine / flagged detail]* — Berger &
   Luckmann: institutionalization is a **reciprocal typification of habitualized actions by types
   of actors**; it **cannot occur in an individual**. So the Graphist / Grapheus / Agonothetes
   are internalized typifications — a *model* of the institution of inquiry carried as sub-roles,
   not institutionalization itself. Conant–Ashby (the good-regulator theorem; requisite variety):
   to manage its own belief-fixation a regulator must model the social division of labor by which
   belief gets fixed — **model-of, never instance-of.** *[flagged]* The Agonothetes-negotiation
   account: it does not decide truth (the peel does, mechanically) — it decides what an outcome
   does to the *UoD's* attested record, resolving non-trivial cases by policy priority, by
   forking the DAG to carry dissent as siblings, or (LLM judge) by choosing among cast votes
   without fabricating; commens-entry is a *separate* act (circulation + uptake), never automatic.
4. **S and A, made measurable** *[ratified spine / flagged coupling]* — the automaton's
   decomposition, now the kytos's: **S** = the UoD-management capability = the interior (already
   instrumented: |M|, the K3 materialization/compression ratio, the peel's super-linear cost
   curve, the decay TTL, admission/retraction rates). **A** = the interaction capability = the
   membrane (the automaton's *missing fifth*, the action arm reached at rung 1 — honestly the
   younger, thinner-instrumented axis: import/export throughput, proposal/doubt rate, horizon
   size, severity-weighted yield). *[flagged]* S and A are **not independent** — coupled by one
   attention budget; the interesting variable is the **allocation** between them, and **poise is
   its reading** (rigidity = S starves A, it stops engaging; thrash = A starves S, it never
   settles).
5. **West, operationalized** *[ratified intent / flagged mapping]* — because each kytos has
   measurable S/A/allocation, a community of kytē can be modeled, "possibly to better understand
   the components of mentation" (author's hedge kept). *[flagged]* the specific conjecture:
   sublinear cost scaling from the shared substrate (the commens as common infrastructure) and
   superlinear production/K-generation from interaction density; fit against exponents in the Q-B
   apportionment experiment. **Conjecture-until-measured**, permanently (measurement is itself an
   ongoing act, never a final).
6. **The rung-boundary** *[flagged formulation]* — sub-role (models a *type of actor*) →
   instance (models the *institution*: good regulator, requisite internal variety) → community
   (genuine reciprocal typification: the institution, the commens). The top jump is a change in
   **kind**, not degree; genuine institutionalization and the commens are community-level
   emergents, not instance properties (→ the Q-B community-of-kytē question).
7. **No "final"** *[ratified]* — the ink/act separation applied to Peircean convergence: to
   scribe "the final opinion" is to lift an act-telos into ink (the dragon-9 reification). What
   survives is convergence as a **direction of the act**, never a nameable terminus. Enforced by
   the machinery: the DAG has no terminal leaf; □ is read *at a time* and stays defeasible as the
   history grows; the ink is **re-runnable act-record** (the polarity gate recomputes every
   verdict, chains replay, §3.3 re-attests) — nothing is ever sealed. The commens is un-closed in
   **space** (no one holds it) and in **time** (no one ends it). Editorial ban-list for the whole
   corpus: **"final," "converges to," "the answer,"** any word that scribes a terminus; the
   register is "tends," "the current best-attested," "open."
8. **Keeping thirdness where it belongs** *[flagged formulation / ratified direction]* — the
   standing statement that the higher-order frontier is **deliberately not crossed**. Thirdness
   (mediation, law, interpretation) is realized **operationally, not representationally**: in the
   teridentity ink (the reduction thesis), in §3.3 attestation (the interpretant-as-prerequisite),
   in the diachronic DAG (would-be / modality), and in the Agonothetes (the game's interpretant)
   — **not** in a higher-order object layer one quantifies over, and **not** in a Gamma tincture.
   Dau Ch. 26's reduction (teridentity + the algebraic operations construct every finitary
   relation) is the proof the ascent is **optional for expressiveness** — only iconicity could
   ever motivate it. Cross-references the new **Mention-ascent** GLOSSARY entry (committed
   2026-07-20).
9. **The honesty guard** *[flagged]* — the automated EPG **does not institutionalize**: three
   LLM roles remain sub-roles within one individual's process (a model of the institution, not an
   institution); branch-on-disagreement is the nearest gesture and is still intra-individual (one
   UoD's history). Claiming otherwise is precisely the B/L reification — mistaking an internalized
   model for the social reality it models. Extends the built / evidenced / conjectured ledger
   (THE_KYTOS §5).
10. **The entailment: connection outward** *[ratified]* — the solitary ceiling sits below the
    community's **by kind, not effort** (some components of knowledge — K2 at community scale, the
    commens itself — are unavailable to one agent). And because the commens is a social construct
    sustained only by participation (§1), connecting outward does not merely *reach* a standing
    commons — it **keeps one in being**; withdrawal is not only self-limiting but *dissolving*. The
    need to connect is a need to **sustain**, not only to access. Publication is the first push of
    the project's membrane into the scholarly commens; the sweep's purpose is **legibility**
    (takeable-up by people who weren't here while it was made), not tidiness. Arisbe-the-project is
    itself a kytos whose UoD is what author and assistant have internalized and attested — growing
    only from what crosses its membrane from a commens neither possesses (the FIDELITY-licensed
    "Arisbe as a proposition in the wider EPG," now with the right ontology under it).
11. **Open verdicts** — the flagged items (§§2, 3-detail, 4-coupling, 5-mapping, 6, 8-formulation,
    9) gathered as a numbered list for the author's rulings, house-style, each as a crisp yes/no or
    a phrasing choice.

**Cross-links out** (in-doc, markdown, no new obligations): World-scroll, Kytos, Mention-ascent,
THE_MEASURE_OF_KNOWLEDGE, BOOTSTRAP_AND_DIRECTED_ENGAGEMENT, MODALITY_WITHOUT_GAMMA,

## Testing / verification

This workstream produces **prose, not code** — no test suite applies. Verification is:

1. **Link integrity** — every intra-repo markdown link in the new/edited docs resolves (a
   grep-based check over the touched files; no dangling `](docs/...)` or `](#anchor)` whose target
   is absent).
2. **Vocabulary conformance** — the touched docs contain no banned terminus-words ("final
   opinion," "converges to [truth]," "the answer") except where the doctrine doc quotes them to
   forbid them; a grep confirms.
3. **Tag completeness** — every section of the doctrine doc carries a `[ratified]`/`[flagged]`
   tag, and every `[flagged]` item appears in the Open-verdicts list (visual check + grep count
   match).
4. **Ripple coverage** — each of the four ripple targets received exactly its specified edit and
   cross-links the doctrine doc.
5. **Protected-core untouched** — no `src/` change; `core_protection_system.py` stays clean
   (trivially, since A edits only docs).
6. **Commit hygiene** — the doctrine doc + ripples in one commit, the ROADMAP rewrite in a
   second, so each is independently reviewable/revertible.
