Rider (b), the author's, as in every prior examination: **fix the reading beforehand,

### The docket, as ruled

1. **Where is the terminal unit?** West's terminal units are structural invariants of
   a *distribution network*. The author's Kandel ruling (`Aplysia`, §3b of
   [FROM_THERMODYNAMICS_TO_SEMIOSIS.md](../../FROM_THERMODYNAMICS_TO_SEMIOSIS.md))
   puts intent 1 at a molecular coincidence detector — far below anything called a
   kytos. If the semiotic terminal unit sits elsewhere than assumed, the nulls were
   foregone.
2. **Is there a network at all?** West's exponents come from *geometry*: space-filling
   fractal networks, invariant terminals, minimized dissipation. Arisbe has no space,
   no flow, no distribution topology. If the mechanism is geometric and there is no
   geometry, the correspondence is an analogy of *results* with no shared mechanism.
3. **Are the condition lists written in the wrong voice?** They enumerate what units
   must be (able to die; fixed quantum). Under the environment-first ruling those are
   *consequences* of a world where maintenance costs and the source depletes.
4. **Does a vector optimand admit an exponent?** If West's universality *depends* on
   scalarity, the "return gift" is a departure rather than an extension.

### The priors

**Q2 — is there a network at all.** *(Taken first: if it lands it reframes the other
three.)*

- **P-W1 — the mechanism prior.** West's exponents derive from three premises used
  *jointly*: space-filling, invariant terminal units, and minimized dissipation

## 2 · Method — rider (a), the panels

The author's rider: *the assistant is not a neutral party* — most of the readings that
make the mapping look shakier were generated in the sitting above — so a panel must be
mandated to **defend** West-in-kytē with equal force, or the session ratifies its own
premises.

Form chosen (author's selection, 2026-08-01): **blind adversarial panels.**

- **Three DEFENSE agents**, each given primary sources only and told to make the
  strongest honest case. Each was **explicitly forbidden** to read the 2026-08-01
  sitting spec or the ▶ NEXT SESSION docket, so that the defense is not a rebuttal
  written downstream of the prosecution's framing.
  - **D1** — reconstruct West's actual derivation from the primary literature (both
    the biological and the socioeconomic branch), then build the mapping table
    premise by premise.
  - **D2** — the literature-facing question, put without reference to Arisbe: *do
    scaling laws with derivable exponents arise in systems with no transport
    geometry?* Charged to find the strongest non-geometric derivation that exists,
    and to report an honest null if there is none.
  - **D3** — the strongest terminal-unit candidate anywhere in the system, at any
    level of the fractal, with each candidate's invariance classified as
    **structural** / **imposed** / **emergent**.
- **Two PROSECUTION agents**, holding the docket as the author wrote it: **P1** on
  Q1+Q2, **P2** on Q3+Q4. Each required to include a section on *how the prosecution
  could be wrong*.
- The assistant referees and **writes neither brief**.

The failure mode this form is designed against: any panel inherits the framing of the
prompt that spawned it. A defense that reads the prosecution first is a rebuttal, not
a defense. Blindness is scoped to the *sitting's skeptical readings* — the defense was
required to read `WEST_IN_KYTE_PROGRAM.md` §8, the project's own self-criticism, since
that is primary evidence rather than framing.

## 3 · Findings

### The Q2 sequence — the escape, and its closing

**VIII.1 — The escape `P-W2` reserved is real, published, and was found by the
defense.** Arbesman, Kleinberg & Strogatz (*Phys. Rev. E* 79:016115, 2009) derive a
West-family superlinear exponent from a hierarchical **social** tree — individuals as
leaves, social distance as the height of the lowest common ancestor, `β = 2 − φ + λ`.
No geography, no transport, no conserved flow. Liben-Nowell et al. (*PNAS* 102:11623,
2005) *prove* that the metric in such arguments reduces to ordinal rank,
"independent of the dimensionality of the space in which people live." **Space is not
logically required for a scaling exponent.** (D2)

**VIII.2 — And the same literature closes it: no space is not no dimension.** Ribeiro
& Rybski (arXiv:2111.08365) show the social-distance and Euclidean models are one
model in two coordinate systems — `φ = γ/D_p`, `r ~ b^(d/D_p)` — with the tree's
branching factor doing full dimensional duty: *"the two approaches, in fact, represent
the same urban phenomenon."* Independently confirmed on the Leskovec CGA model, whose
`a = 2 − log_b c` is δ/d in ultrametric clothing. The obligation this places on any
defense is therefore not to deny the metric but to **exhibit the effective
dimension**. (D2)

**VIII.3 — Two blind panels, two limits of one equation, the same number: β = 1
exactly.** Bettencourt's interaction density is `δ = H/(D(D+H))`. D1 reached the
result through `H → 0`: `MarkBoard` is flat, every published mark equally reachable by
every unit, so reach does not grow with community extent, and Bettencourt's own Table
1 states *"agglomeration effects vanish as H → 0."* D2 reached it through `D → ∞`: a
system where any unit addresses any other at uniform cost is the infinite-dimensional
limit. Both give δ = 0, α = 1, `A_n ∝ N`, and `Y = GN²/A_n ∝ N`. **This is not an
absence of evidence for an exponent. It is a derived prediction that there is none**,
from the mapping's own primary source, and six runs of nulls are what that prediction
looks like from the inside. (D1 and D2, independently)

**VIII.4 — There is no expression in Arisbe whose value could be 3/4.** WBE's result
is literally `a = − ln n / ln(γβ²)` — a ratio of logarithms of two per-level scale
factors, γ from space-filling and β from area-preserving branching. The C-series has
one flat board and no levels; the E-series had two. With no branching ratio and no
per-level cross-section, **β and γ are undefined**, and premise 5 — area-preserving
versus area-increasing branching, the sole discriminator between a = 3/4 and a = 1 —
has no occupant at all. (D1)

**VIII.5 — The prosecution's three-premise audit, held against the *organism*
register the failing rows actually use.** Conserved flowing quantity: **absent** —
`Field.deliver` is a pure function of (seed, domain, round) holding no mutable state,
and `MarkBoard` is append-only in both directions, so consumption by one aperture
removes nothing from another. *Signs are non-rival; blood is not.* Branching
distribution topology: **absent** — `Unit.read` returns everything published minus the
unit's own, with no addressing, distance, hops or levels; the ask channel's own
docstring concedes that *what a question cannot do is choose whom to ask.*
Dissipation minimized over that topology: **absent** — the C-series refuses even to
sum ("kinds are kept apart and never summed"), and E3's hill-climb minimizes over a
set partition of folders using a meter that charges Σ\|M\|, model size rather than
work. (P1)

**VIII.6 — The headline measurement is an identity, and the programme already said
so.** β_mono ≈ 1.277 versus β_fed ≈ 1.025 is a six-point fit to Σ\|M\| over a partition
count, where a monolith running R rounds pays ≈ c·R²/2 and a federation splitting them
across F+1 members pays ≈ c·R²/2(F+1). The design additionally set `R = 25·(F+1)`,
making rounds an affine function of the abscissa. `WEST_E2_LOG.md` already retracts
the interpretation. (D1, P1)

### The referee's own prior, corrected

**VIII.7 — `P-W1` was over-strict, and the defense is what corrected it.** The
pre-registered test demanded a conserved flowing quantity, a branching topology, and
minimized dissipation. Those three are required for the **metabolic** family (WBE;
Banavar, Maritan & Rinaldo 1999 derive D/(D+1) from an explicit continuity argument).
They are **not** required for the socioeconomic family. The minimal premise set common
to every derivation in the `β = 2 − θ` family is: (1) N countable invariant units;
(2) output from pairwise interaction, giving the N² numerator; (3) **a scale-free
accessibility structure** — a cost coordinate with the count of units reachable at
cost *d* growing as a fixed power or exponential; (4) a bounded per-unit budget; (5)
decay of interaction with cost at exponent **θ < 1**. Item (3) is the irreducible one:
it is what "dimension" *means*, and it need not be spatial, but it must exist and be
scale-free. **Had the defense not corrected this, the examination would have convicted
on the wrong charge.** Arisbe fails the corrected test as well — it has no accessibility
gradient — so the verdict is unchanged and its grounds are narrower and firmer. (D2,
correcting the referee)

**VIII.8 — Terminal-unit invariance is assumed in West too, so "we imposed it" is not
by itself the defect.** WBE: *"the important assumption, the second above, that the
terminal units (capillaries) are invariant."* Bettencourt: *"I assume that the
transverse dimension of the smallest network units, s\*, is independent of N."*
Neither derives it. What West has is not a derivation but **independent empirical
warrant** — capillaries really are the same size in a shrew and a whale. The honest
statement is therefore: *Arisbe's terminal-unit invariance sits at the same logical
station as West's and at a worse evidential station.* The narrower damning fact is not
the assignment but that the E-series once **reported its own assignment as a measured
signal** (±0.9% clustering) — already withdrawn in `WEST_E2_LOG.md`. (D1 and P1,
independently; both correcting the referee's first reading)

### The terminal unit

**VIII.9 — A genuine invariant quantum exists, and it is not the unit.** The
**proposition as unit of account** — one claim, one standing, one verdict, one
inscription, one act, ever — is invariant across every axis the C-series can vary, and
its invariance is neither a fixture constant nor a modeller's assignment. Four
mechanisms built at different times for different reasons converged on it, each with

measurement** rather than chosen — charging a standing bet every round made misses grow
with the *square* of run length while hits grew linearly, so a true law could lose
money, and *a statistic that worsens with run length for a true law is measuring run
length, not knowledge.* (D3)

**VIII.10 — What that quantum lacks is metabolism, not invariance.** It has a capacity
and **no rate**: `publish` and `challenge` are uncapped by explicit decision, with

**VIII.11 — The size sweep is structurally unreachable, so invariance could not have

magnitude of N." No exponent is estimable from 4 → 6, whatever else is right. (D3)

**VIII.12 — The escape upward is closed.** Relocating the terminal unit to the
community does not repair the mis-mapping: `MarkBoard` is per-community and sealed,
holding no reference to another board. West's terminal is terminal *within* a supply
network that feeds it, and **nothing feeds an Arisbe community.** (D3)

**VIII.13 — One premise is genuinely occupied *and was discovered*.** Widening a
unit's aperture from two domains to three was measured **degenerate** — all 168 true

eyes on a domain is more units, not wider ones*. That is West's architectural

**VIII.14 — The nulls trace to chosen constants, not to the world.** Typification

Under Q1's own terms: **foregone.** (P1)

### The vector optimand

**VIII.19 — Q4's docket premise is false: West's universality does not depend on
scalarity.** A scalar objective is genuinely present in WBE — the Lagrangian
`F = W + λV_b + Σλ_k N_k l_k³ + λ_M M`, reduced to minimizing impedance Z — but it does
not select the exponent. WBE's own sentence: *"this derivation of the a = 3/4 law is
essentially a geometric one."* Dodds, Rothman & Weitz (2001) **proved** that
space-filling alone yields γ with no minimization, and that actually performing the
minimization gives Murray's law and **a = 1**. Savage, Deeds & Fontana (2008)
**proved** the Lagrange system under-determined. Banavar et al. (1999) minimize a
*different* scalar and still reach 3/4; Banavar et al. (2010) reach it with **no
Lagrange multiplier anywhere**. Three derivations minimizing three different things —
impedance, blood volume, nothing — converge on the same exponent, which they could not
do if the optimand selected it. **The geometry selects it.** (P2)

**VIII.20 — Q4 nonetheless returns *departure*, on narrower and firmer grounds.** No
known derivation obtains a power-law exponent from a vector-valued objective. Shoval
et al. (2012) prove a **shape** theorem — the Pareto front is the convex hull of the
archetypes; Sheftel et al. (2013) add hyperbolic segments and a proved
**non-identifiability**; Szekely et al. (2015) use the front to argue the ¼-law is an
**artifact** of regressing a line through a triangle, dissolving the exponent rather
than deriving it; Sun & Zhang (2021) measure ParTI's Type-I error above 0.75 under
neutral evolution. The structural reason this is not accidental: a power law is
scale-free, with its content in the exponent; a Pareto hull has its content in finitely
many vertices, and **hull-taking is affine-equivariant — it commutes with axis
rescaling and therefore cannot manufacture an exponent.** (P2)

**VIII.21 — The guard tension is narrower than the docket assumed, and the project may
already be doing the licit thing without naming it.** `THE_MEASURE_OF_KNOWLEDGE.md`
forbids *aggregating the item index away*; it does **not** forbid comparing within an
item. Scalarizing K1–K4 within an item to obtain a West-style optimand is therefore not
forbidden — and the E-series' actual construction, *minimize cost at equal K2*, is an
**ε-constraint scalarization**, which is provably complete (reaches the whole front
with no convexity assumption). (P2)

**VIII.22 — But the "frozen-landscape shadow" concedes a scalarization without naming
it.** `WEST_METHODS_NOTE.md` proposes "a scaling *manifold* whose frozen-landscape
shadow shows up as the observed exponent." A shadow is a projection; a projection of a
vector objective is a scalarization; and under linear weights the cost is exact and
severe — only the convex hull is reachable (Geoffrion's necessity direction requires
convexity), the weight→optimum map is non-injective and jumps discontinuously across
cavities, and **the weight ratio *is* the front's local slope** (Das & Dennis). So the
shadow is set by the freezing, not by the manifold. (P2)

**VIII.23 — And the gift is aimed at the wrong organ.** If the exponent comes from
space-filling plus area-preserving branching plus an invariant terminal unit, what a
kytos would have to supply West is a **geometry**, not an optimand. The vector-optimand
offer does not touch the mechanism it proposes to extend. (P2)
