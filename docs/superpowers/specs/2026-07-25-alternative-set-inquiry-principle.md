# AlternativeSet Inquiry Principle: Discovery of Distinctions

**Status:** Design principle for Item 4 Phase 2, Tasks 4–6  
**Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Grounding:** Peircean pragmatism + bootstrap directed engagement

---

## Core Principle

**Do not pre-filter alternatives by assumed consequences. Discover distinctions through inquiry, and let the discovery of distinctions expand both S (sign-space) and A (action-space).**

In Peircean terms: *A genuine doubt requires tracing conceivable differences in outcomes. We don't know which differences matter until we ask what paths each alternative leads to.*

---

## The Problem with Pre-Filtering

If we attempt to filter alternatives to "only materially different ones" *before* inquiry:

1. We assume we already know what "material" means
2. We beg the question: "which differences matter?"
3. We truncate the system's capacity to discover new distinctions
4. We lock in a fixed S and A instead of letting them grow

**Example of the trap:**
- We detect alternatives {TRUE, FALSE}
- We assume: "Both are materially different, so keep both"
- We never discover: actually TRUE leads to two distinct revision paths (TRUE-high-K2 vs TRUE-low-K2)
- We never expand S with {K2-stability} or A with {K2-conditional-choice}

---

## The Inquiry Cycle (Correct Approach)

**Two Sources of Refinement:**
- **Internal:** Trace consequences, discover distinctions
- **External:** Receive input from the membrane (teaching, correction, novelty)

**Key constraint:** Each kyte is finite and mortal. S and A are bounded. The kyte doesn't expand unboundedly; it *refines* and *reallocates* within its limits, or relies on the membrane to compensate for what it can't perceive/do.

```
1. DETECT ALTERNATIVES (unfiltered)
   └─ Record what we observe, without pre-judging consequences
   └─ Source: internal detection OR external membrane input

2. GENERATE META-QUESTIONS
   └─ "If I select alternative A, what follows?"
      "If I select alternative B, what follows?"
      "How do the paths differ?"

3. TRACE INQUIRY PATHS (internal consequence)
   └─ Run the peel, materialize M, measure K1/K2/K3/K4
   └─ Build proof trees showing consequences
   └─ Discover finer distinctions in outcomes

4. LISTEN TO MEMBRANE (external input)
   └─ Is there a correction from outside? A teaching moment?
   └─ A contradiction the world presents?
   └─ Record external input with warrant < 1.0 (received, not derived)

5. REFINE SIGN-SPACE (S) — within bounded capacity
   └─ Internal: vocabulary from discovered distinctions
   └─ External: vocabulary from external input
   └─ Constraint: This kyte has finite S-capacity. May need to decay/forget to make room.
   └─ Heterogeneity: Different kytes refine S differently (different bounds, different priorities)
   └─ Example (internal): discover {K2-high, K2-low} → specialize in K2-stability (and deprioritize unused)
   └─ Example (external): world teaches "perspective X" → integrate it (may require forgetting something else)

6. REFINE ACTION-SPACE (A) — within bounded capacity
   └─ Internal: responses to discovered distinctions
   └─ External: responses suggested by external input
   └─ Constraint: This kyte has finite A-capacity. May need to specialize/decay unused actions.
   └─ Heterogeneity: Different kytes specialize in different actions based on their nature
   └─ Example (internal): can now condition actions on K2 stability (drop simpler heuristics)
   └─ Example (external): world shows "approach X works" → adopt it (may abandon prior strategy)

7. UPDATE WARRANT — from both sources
   └─ Internal distinctions: warrant increases with trace evidence
   └─ External input: warrant starts lower (received, not derived), updates with integration
   └─ If distinctions/inputs align → high warrant (robust)
   └─ If distinctions/inputs conflict → mid warrant (requires further inquiry)
   └─ If spurious (no consequences) → low warrant (collapse or reject)

8. MANAGE DISUSE-DECAY — rational bounded optimization
   └─ Vocabulary/actions not used in recent inquiry decay (freed capacity for new learning)
   └─ This is not loss; it's resource management. The kyte is finite.
   └─ Track what decayed (for succession — next generation may need it)

9. REPEAT WITH REFINED SENSITIVITY (within this kyte's bounds)
   └─ Refined S/A enables detection of finer distinctions *in this kyte's domain*
   └─ Different kytes detect different distinctions (heterogeneous bounds)
   └─ Next cycle is more receptive to external input (refinement enables listening)

10. SUCCESSION: Pass refined S/A to next generation
    └─ This kyte's S/A die with it (mortality)
    └─ Knowledge survives via transmission to successors (transformed by their different bounds)
    └─ Next-generation kyte specializes differently (different capacity, different interaction density)
```

**Key insight:** The system is NOT closed, and NOT unbounded. It's in **bounded dialogue with its membrane**. 
- Each individual kyte is finite and mortal (constrained S/A, disuse-decay, eventual death)
- Novelty is not only discovered internally; it's *received* from the membrane (teaching, correction, complementary perspectives)
- The system *as a whole* (many kytes, dense interaction, succession over generations) accumulates and refines knowledge
- But each individual kyte remains bounded: it specializes, not omniscience

---

## Avoiding Begging the Question

**The key constraint:** Never assume consequences; always derive them.

| Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|
| "These alternatives are materially different because..." | "Let's trace what each alternative leads to and measure the difference" |
| Filter first, then ask | Ask first, then filter based on what we learn |
| S and A are fixed and infinite | S and A are bounded; refine within limits, decay unused capacity |
| Warrant is preset | Warrant updates as we discover distinctions |
| All kytes learn the same distinctions | Different kytes specialize (bounded heterogeneity) |
| Knowledge is permanent | Individual kytes are mortal; knowledge survives via transmission |

## Implementation in Tasks 4–6

Each task must explicitly:

1. **Record alternatives as-is** — do not filter or pre-judge
2. **Generate meta-questions** — "what consequences follow from each?"
3. **Trace inquiry paths** — run the system (peel, materialize, measure)
4. **Discover finer distinctions** — compare outcomes, find what differs
5. **Refine S and A** — document new vocabulary and actions this kyte learned
   - Within its bounded capacity
   - What must be forgotten/decayed to make room?
   - How does this differ from what other kytes would learn (heterogeneity)?
6. **Track disuse-decay** — what was deprioritized and why?
7. **Report warrant updates** — based on actual differences discovered
8. **Record for succession** — what did this kyte learn that could be transmitted to next generation?

**Example (Task 4: UNKNOWN verdicts):**

```
Input: UNKNOWN verdict on (white "Alba")

Stage 1: Detect Alternatives (unfiltered)
  alternatives = {TRUE, FALSE}

Stage 2: Meta-questions
  Q1: "If TRUE: what M-revisions follow?"
  Q2: "If FALSE: what M-revisions follow?"
  Q3: "Do these differ materially?"

Stage 3: Trace paths
  Path-TRUE: peel(M, TRUE) → verdict → revise_with_disposition(...) → new M
  Path-FALSE: peel(M, FALSE) → verdict → revise_with_disposition(...) → new M
  Measure: K1, K2, K3, K4 on both outcomes

Stage 4: Discover distinctions
  Finding: Path-TRUE leads to M with K2=0.7; Path-FALSE leads to M with K2=0.3
  New distinction: K2-stability differs significantly
  Implication: These are materially different alternatives

Stage 5: Refine S and A (within bounded capacity)
  S refines: add "K2-divergence" to vocabulary
    Constraint: This kyte's S has limited capacity. What will decay to make room?
    Decision: Deprioritize "stability-ignoring-strategies" (now unused) → decay
  A refines: can now condition actions on K2 trajectory
    Constraint: This kyte's A has limited capacity. What actions are unused?
    Decision: Specialize in K2-trajectory-optimization; abandon uniform strategies

Stage 6: Disuse-decay
  Decayed: "stability-ignoring-strategies" (S) and "uniform-action-policies" (A)
  Reason: No longer used given new K2 distinction
  Record: For succession, next kyte may find these useful in different context

Stage 7: Update warrant
  Initial warrant: 1.0 (speculative)
  After inquiry: 1.0 (material difference confirmed by trace)
  External validation: Did membrane (other kytes, world) agree? (recorded separately)

Record in UoD:

    s_decayed=["stability-ignoring-strategies"],  # freed capacity
    a_expansion=["K2-conditional-choice"], # refined within bounded A
```

## Distinction vs. Spurious Alternative

**Material distinction:** The alternatives lead to different outcomes in S or A
- Different verdicts
- Different M-revisions
- Different K-measures
- Different inferences or actions

**Spurious alternative:** The alternatives make no conceivable difference
- Both lead to the same M
- Both lead to the same K-measures
- Both would trigger identical actions
- → Warrant drops to 0; collapse to one

**Intermediate:** The distinction is uncertain or partially material
- Some outcomes differ, some are identical
- → Warrant stays mid-range (0.5) until further inquiry

## Why This Matters

1. **Avoids circular reasoning** — we don't assume what we intend to prove
2. **Discovers new distinctions** — within this kyte's bounded capacity
3. **Refines S and A rationally** — bounded optimization via reallocation, not unbounded expansion
4. **Honest warrant** — based on actual discovered consequences, not assumptions
5. **Enables finer responses** — the kyte specializes in what fits its nature and bounds
6. **Explains disuse-decay** — not loss, but rational capacity management
7. **Enables succession** — refined knowledge is transmitted to next generation (transformed by their different bounds)
8. **Supports system-level learning** — many bounded kytes + dense interaction → system-wide refinement

This is how individual kytes don't just *store* knowledge, but *discover* it. And discovery refines what each kyte can perceive and do within its limits. The system as a whole becomes smarter through interaction density and succession, even though each individual is finite and mortal.

---

## Connection to Bootstrap, West's Scaling, and Mortality

**Individual kyte (bounded):**
- S and A refined through inquiry (internal) + reception (external)
- Disuse-decay is rational (finite capacity)
- Mortal (the kyte dies; S/A die with it)
- Heterogeneous (different kytes have different bounds, different specializations)

**System as a whole (unbounded over time):**
- Interaction density drives refinement (West's scaling principle)
- Knowledge persists via succession (dies with individual, reborn transformed in successor)
- Heterogeneity is adaptive (different kytes fill different ecological niches)
- Mortality is necessary (prevents infinite accumulation; forces specialization and forgetting)

AlternativeSet inquiry *is* how individual kytes refine:
- **S refines** when we discover finer distinctions (within this kyte's capacity)
- **A refines** when we enable finer responses (within this kyte's capacity)
- **Disuse-decay** frees capacity for new learning
- **External input** compensates for what this kyte can't perceive alone
- **Succession** transmits refined knowledge to next generation

---

## Summary

**For Tasks 4–6: Inquiry is bounded optimization, not unbounded growth.**

Do not ask "which alternatives are materially different?" Ask instead: "What paths do these alternatives lead to? What distinctions emerge when we trace those paths? What must this kyte forget to make room? What does the membrane teach?"

Let the distinctions you discover *be* your evidence for materiality. Let the refined vocabulary and actions that emerge *be* your measure of specialization. Let disuse-decay *be* your evidence of rational capacity management.

This honors Peirce and West: inquiry is the engine of refinement within bounded systems; system-level knowledge grows through dense interaction across many bounded, heterogeneous, mortal kytes.
