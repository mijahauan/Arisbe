# Expanding the slate — candidate recipients beyond the first four

**Status: proposed slate (2026-07-27, Sitting B2), awaiting the author's
ruling at the Share sitting. No skeleton is drafted for anyone below until
ruled in.**

**Why this document.** The author's observation, mid-sitting 2026-07-27: the
AlternativeSet turned out to be the joint where six trains of thought meet
(erotetics · attention economy · modality · the deliberative interval ·
mention-ascent · hope), and the use cases were defined *before* West, Berger &
Luckmann, Graeber & Wengrow, the kyto-pathologies, the ethics work, or that
confluence existed — so both the audience and the use-case catalogue are due
an expansion. The use-case half is acted on in ARISBE_IN_PRACTICE (the three
registers + the population-experimenter use case). This document holds the
audience half: which *kinds* of scholar now have something to test here, and
who might stand for each kind.

The first four (Sowa · Dau · West · Pietarinen) cover the formalization
lineage and one measured tributary. The confluence adds at least these:

## 1 · Erotetic logic — the question as first-class object

**Kind:** logicians of questions (the Hamblin / Belnap / Wiśniewski line —
the concordance map's row 9). **Candidate:** Andrzej Wiśniewski (Inferential
Erotetic Logic). **The proposition for them:** the question's *career* —
minted from an UNKNOWN verdict as an {atom, denial} pair, traced for the
materiality of each answer (a vector, never a scalar), priced by an attention
economy, settled only by a licensed step the record can cite — offered as an
*operational* erotetics: what their tradition formalized statically, with an
economics their tradition named as missing. **They could refute:** whether
the {atom, denial} pair is an adequate question-semantics at all against IEL's
richer erotetic inferences. **Must stand behind the link:**
[ALTERNATIVE_SET_INTELLECTUAL_HISTORY](../ALTERNATIVE_SET_INTELLECTUAL_HISTORY.md)
(their tradition is told there at length), the `swan_alternatives` exemplar,
ARISBE_FOR_SCHOLARS' second storey.

## 2 · Social epistemology — uptake, injustice, and the record

**Kind:** epistemologists of testimony and credibility. **Candidate:**
Miranda Fricker (epistemic injustice; her uptake duty is the load-bearing
bridge in THE_MEASURE_OF_KNOWLEDGE's ethics section — cited, not decorative).
**The proposition for them:** a system in which the graph is judged, never
the grapher — competence-never-worth as an enforced category-fact, the record
as public reason, and the honest concession that fair access is *not*
derivable from the machinery (the Fidelity examinations' own finding). **They
could refute:** whether the marks doctrine + uptake framing is a faithful use
of the concept or a flattering borrowing. **Must stand behind the link:**
THE_MEASURE_OF_KNOWLEDGE §4, FIDELITY_AND_DEPARTURES + the examination
record, THE_COMMENS_AND_THE_COMMUNITY.

## 3 · Complexity science — populations of bounded units

**Kind:** the scaling/complexity community around West (Santa Fe-shaped),
beyond West himself. **Candidate:** to the author's judgment — the case does
not hang on one name. **The proposition for them:** the population
laboratory: deterministic populations of bounded inference units with
per-unit parameters (decay, budget, temperament dial) and two-scale readouts
(unit ledgers below, granularity/basin structure above), with the E-series as
the first pre-registered study and the basin-map findings (granularity
converges, bucketing fragments, balance strands) as the first results. **They
could refute:** the internal-validity risks the logs themselves name —
synthetic corpus, one landscape, modeled concurrency. **Must stand behind
the link:** [WEST_METHODS_NOTE](../WEST_METHODS_NOTE.md), the run logs,
ARISBE_IN_PRACTICE Part IV.

**⚠ Held with the West letter (2026-07-28).** The audit that stopped that
letter ([WEST_IN_KYTE_PROGRAM](../WEST_IN_KYTE_PROGRAM.md) §8) binds this row
too: the population laboratory described above does not yet exist in that form
— the units neither communicate nor reason, and the terminal-unit framing
mis-maps. Approach this community only behind a corrected experiment.

## 4 · Anthropology / archaeology — plurality without a ladder

**Kind:** readers of *The Dawn of Everything*. **Candidate:** David Wengrow
(Graeber d. 2020). **The proposition for them:** the project adopted their
*negative* claim only (many viable settlements, no developmental ladder),
then *measured* a plurality (the multi-basin landscape) in a domain they
never claimed — and records "politics = coordinated basin-crossing" as a
finding about the model, explicitly not about history. **They could refute:**
whether that reading borrows their authority for something their evidence
does not touch. **Must stand behind the link:** the concordance map's G&W
row (negative-claim-only, verbatim), THE_KYTOS §4's reading disciplines,
WEST_METHODS_NOTE §5.

## 5 · Active inference — the deliberative record

**Kind:** the free-energy/predictive-processing community. **Candidate:**
to the author's judgment (the named principals are heavily addressed;
the accessible readers of that community may be the better door). **The
proposition for them:** the closest formal neighbor, with the sharpest
difference held up front: their updates are a gradient flow that keeps no
inspectable chain; Arisbe's are recorded, licensed steps — plus the
membrane/Markov-blanket convergence and the three-valued verdict's
abstention/error distinction a scalar surprisal collapses. **They could
refute:** whether the deliberative-record contrast is real or a difference of
description level. **Must stand behind the link:** the concordance map's
Friston row, BOOTSTRAP_AND_DIRECTED_ENGAGEMENT (the action arm honestly
absent).

## 6 · The Peirce-scholarship cluster, widened

Two natural additions beside Pietarinen, both already load-bearing citations:
**Frederik Stjernfelt** (operational iconicity — the banner's source; the
correspondence invariant is his concept made a runtime check) and **Sun-Joo
Shin** (*The Iconic Logic of Peirce's Graphs* — part of the formalization
lineage the chapter sets apart; her reading of EG's iconic advantages is
exactly what the drawn-form machinery operationalizes or fails to). Both
would receive the Pietarinen letter's evidence base with a different §2.

## Kinds considered and deliberately *not* proposed yet

- **Sociology of knowledge (Berger & Luckmann's line):** both principals
  deceased; no living addressee stands out, and the tradition is served by
  the intellectual-history chapter rather than a letter.
- **Ethics of technology / machine ethics:** premature — the rung-2 ethics
  floor is design-stage; a letter would cite work that is not yet peels
  played.
- **Education / tutoring research:** premature for the same reason (the
  tutor loop is design-only, and the project's own doc says so).
