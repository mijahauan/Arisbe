# Letter skeleton — Geoffrey West

**Status: internal skeleton (2026-07-27), not for sending.**
**Why him:** *Scale*'s doubt — do aggregated units obey discoverable scaling
laws, and what does the unit's economics determine? — is one the project took
seriously enough to measure. The letter reports measurements run against
pre-registered priors in a domain his book does not cover (knowledge systems),
and offers a conjecture back.

---

## 1 · The debt (open with what is his)

- The question is his: whether a federation of units and a monolith differ in
  *lawful, measurable* ways as they scale — cost exponents, interior optima,
  the economics of the unit. Arisbe's "kytos" (its recurring
  membrane-and-loop unit of inquiry) gave the question a new substrate: units
  whose metabolism is **knowledge maintenance** (a four-component measure:
  track record, durability, compression, use) rather than energy.

## 2 · The proposition, in his vocabulary

Knowledge systems built from bounded inquiry-units appear to have a **scaling
economics** with discoverable structure — and it was cheap to measure once the
units kept honest ledgers. The E-series (five experiments, each with priors
pre-registered before the run; refuted priors kept on the record):

- **E1** — federation vs monolith at equal durability: **~5.2× cheaper**
  (36,097 vs 188,039), and the federation retained more (|M| 1,367 vs 752).
- **E2** — size sweep: **β_monolith = 1.277 vs β_federation = 1.025** (fit
  cost ∝ F^β); a **25× cost spread** at the largest size traceable to
  coordinator scan discipline alone; terminal-unit cost invariant to three
  significant figures (max/min 1.0012) across an 8× size range (his
  "invariant terminal unit," observed).
- **E2b** — imposed partitions: an **interior optimum N\* = 3** (not
  finest-possible, not monolith).
- **E3** — *endogenous* partitioning (the system re-partitioning itself under
  its own economics): all walks converge to the same **granularity** (N = 3)
  but different partitions — "the optimum is a granularity, not a
  partition."
- **E3b/E3c** — the basin map (36 structured starts): granularity converges
  absolutely; bucketing fragments (**19 distinct local optima from the 36
  starts; 21 known after E3c's perturbation rider**); cost concentrates (one
  family holds **75% of attractor mass within 1.4% of the floor**); and
  **balance strands** — the balanced partition sits in a positive-measure
  *dear* basin (single-folder symmetry-breaking escaped it only 1 time in
  3), while asymmetry funnels to the cheap family.

**The return-gift, offered strictly as a conjecture:** energy is scalar at
the *supply* layer, but the *allocation-toward-purposes* layer of these units
is **vectorial** (the knowledge measure) and landscape-changing (niche
construction). If so, a scaling **manifold** rather than a scalar exponent
may be the right object, and the observed exponent the frozen-landscape
shadow of it. Conjecture-until-measured; we state the measurement we think
would decide it and invite him to replace it with a better one.

## 3 · The standing caution (state it before he must)

The exponents characterize *a* fitness landscape a modeler imposed, within
one level, on the economic axis — **never an arrow of social development**
(the project's examined no-teleology discipline; also its Graeber & Wengrow
row: plurality of viable settlements, a negative claim only).
