## 2 · The proposition, in his vocabulary

The one thing we would most want his eyes on: the **runtime-attested
correspondence invariant**. Every (EGI, drawing) pair served, saved, or
admitted to the corpus passes `attest_correspondence` — containment,
incidence, ligature-crossing read off the *drawn shape* — and the system
**refuses** any pair that does not correspond. The linear and graphical forms
are two projections of one coordinate-free object, provably, at runtime, per
request. To our knowledge no CG/EG tool has enforced this; his judgment on
whether that claim survives, and on what the CG lineage would demand of it,
is the judgment we cannot render ourselves.

Secondary: the endoporeutic game he described is **playable and automated**
here — hot-seat in a browser arena, and autonomously (three LLM roles under
an incorruptible mechanical peel: *the LLM argues, the calculus decides*),
with the model M developing through play against live sources (Wikidata).
