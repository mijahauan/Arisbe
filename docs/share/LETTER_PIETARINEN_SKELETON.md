## 2 · The proposition, in his vocabulary

1. **The Agonothetes.** Peirce's game terminates at a dyad; his semiotic is
   triadic. We name the third, telic function — the interpretant of the
   game-as-semiosis — and since 2026-07 it is sharpened to a *ratified role
   account*: **two players only, no move-by-move referee** (legality belongs
   to the calculus), and the Agonothetes **not a player** but the maker of
   two **risked choices** — before, the choice of M; after, the selection of
   a fate from an agreed taxonomy. We believe this is faithful to Peirce; we
   would like to be told if it is not.
2. **Modality without Gamma — stated so he can refute it.** Arisbe draws no
   modal mark: the diachronic branching record itself is the drawn Kripke
   frame (◇ = some legal trajectory scribes it; □ = every one does). We know
   this runs *against* Ma & Pietarinen 2018's sound-and-complete broken-cut
   calculi; our claim is deliberately **adequacy, not completeness**, the
   choice architectural rather than a refutation of Gamma — and his judgment
   on whether that positioning is honest is invited.
3. **Mention-ascent — Peirce's graph-of-a-graph made native.** The dotted
   oval as a core-carried quotation device (sorted name + graph-valued area,
   mention-never-use, conservativity-gated), with hypostatic abstraction as
   the ascent operator; the un-asserted third tense ("present without
   force") that lets a withdrawn law remain on the sheet as exhibit.
4. **The question as first-class object.** An UNKNOWN verdict now begins a
   career: an interrogative record citing the peel that raised it, traced
   for consequences, priced by an attention economy, settled only by
   licensed ink — inquiry's *open questions* carried in the record with the
   same discipline as its assertions. We read this as completing the
   pragmatist account of doubt operationally; he may read it otherwise.
