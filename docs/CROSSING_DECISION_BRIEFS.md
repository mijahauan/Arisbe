## Brief A — the comprehension floor

**The question.** Which graph-collections may the second-order layer quantify
over / be asserted over without paradox — the "which comprehensions are legal"
control that Peirce, who drew second-order claims but never disciplined their
formation, did not have (SECOND_ORDER_FRONTIER: "the moment a dotted line
quantifies over propositions … Russell / Grelling / Liar loom, and Peirce has
no answer"). It is the one genuinely *borrowed* piece of the crossing.

**What is already fixed.** The harness law **S1** implements one floor today:
a *predicative* quote (naming a strictly-lower graph) is always well-formed; an
*impredicative* quote (naming the host's own level or itself) is well-formed
**only enclosed** under ≥1 cut — never flat on the sheet (the dragon-9
discipline: a self-assessment is at most ◇, never asserted flat as □). S4 names
whatever the floor forbids; the falsifier tests bite. The code marks the choice
honestly: "one chosen, defensible floor … the choice is the author's."

**The options, as the record surveys them.**

1. **Predicative-with-enclosure-escape** (the default; ACA₀-like on the
   reverse-mathematics ladder). Peirce-continuous ("vary the sort, not the
   rules"), Quine-proof, keeps completeness, no set-theoretic hostages.
2. **Full / impredicative comprehension** — surveyed and rejected on warrant
   grounds, not consistency: full semantics loses the complete calculus and is
   *non-absolute* (a second-order validity can be equivalent to CH — "Cohen's
   forcing changes which second-order sentences are valid"), exactly the
   unearned warrant the project refuses elsewhere.
3. **Ramified types / Tarski tower** — taken as *discipline*, not surface: it
   fails the drawable test in native form; only the sortal well-formedness rule
   is kept.
4. **Higher rungs (ATR₀, Π¹₁-CA₀)** — deferred, not rejected: the ladder names
   them so a later climb is "a measured step, not a leap."

**The refinement riding with the default** (dragon 12): split-level — **S1
stratifies *formation*** (what may be drawn flat) while **evaluation stays
K3-partial** (`Verdict3`; ungrounded instances read UNKNOWN rather than
crashing or being banned). Groundedness, not syntactic self-mention checking,
is the real invariant (Yablo).

**The categorial footing.** Predicativity is the honest bootstrap drawn as
cuts — the modeller's Thirdness stands one enclosure above the modelled.
**Henkin semantics is unlimited semiosis respected**: quantifiers range over
the interpretants actually constructed, never the final interpretant as a
surveyable object — the *Peircean* choice, not the timid one. Conservativity
over the Dau core is the leak-prevention theorem: "Dau remains the guarantor,"
made testable.

**Foreclosures and escape costs.** Taking the default forecloses
full-semantics categoricity claims (a deliberate trade) and flat impredicative
assertion (enclosed remains legal); it does **not** foreclose second-order
claims at the model-checking register (finite M, the peel — safe territory).
Escaping later is designed cheap: the floor is a parameter at S1 (today latent
— the default predicate is hard-coded; a swap touches S1 and its tests only),
and any climb re-opens exactly one obligation: **re-demonstrate conservativity
over the Dau core** (the crossing invariant).

**Verdict form (A).** The author affirms / amends:

> **A1.** The comprehension floor is **predicative with the enclosure escape**
> (S1 as built) — ratified as doctrine, not merely as the harness default.
> **A2.** The split-level refinement is adopted: stratified *formation*,
> K3-partial *evaluation* (UNKNOWN for the ungrounded).
> **A3.** **Conservativity over the Dau core is the standing crossing
> invariant** — when the layer opens, a corpus-level test must demonstrate the
> quotation layer licenses no new first-order assertion.
> **A4.** The ladder above stays named-and-ordered; climbing requires a
> demonstrated need and re-proof of A3.

## Brief B — how much to open the core

**The question.** Does the quotation/sort layer stay an **overlay** beside
`egi_core_dau` forever (the `reference_node` precedent), or does the core gain
a **native graph-valued node** plus a reader that recovers the sort from the
drawing? The record reduces the whole decision to **one hinge: S3 (read-back)**
— can the picture *be* the second-order proposition, read back off geometry
alone? Everything else follows.

**The graded options.**

- **Option A — overlay-forever.** Zero protected-core risk; ships today; S1,
  S2, S4, S5 all check on the overlay. Its one permanent cost: **S3 cannot be
  built faithfully** — an overlay is by definition not in the drawing, so
  read-back stays injected / skip-named, an honestly-named gap. "A is the whole
  frontier *minus the crossing*."
- **Option B-min — a sort on the incidence.** A second-order sort for a line +
  a graph-valued area; additive to the tuple, but a genuine protected-core edit
  (authorised), a new immutability constructor (`with_quotation`), the six Dau
  rules made sort-preserving, and the dotted-line/oval conventions *committed*
  (not chrome). **S3 becomes buildable** — second-order assertion becomes
  sound-and-attested rather than sound-with-a-named-gap.
- **Option B-full — a native graph-valued element kind** (`ν` can hook a blank
  to it) — the one Peirce's dotted oval most literally is. The larger step;
  the record orders it strictly after B-min.

A → B-min → B-full is a **strict-prefix ladder**: nothing built at a lower
rung is thrown away; the only hard-to-reverse act is the core edit at the
B-min threshold. And B is **one decision with the reference-node increment-2
use/mention fork** — decide them together, pay the core-opening cost once.

**The two nominees** (what B would be exercised on — the first *asserted*
second-order claim):

1. **`(forces s φ)`** — imported, exact. State-name + quotation + a relation
   whose semantics are *defined* by existing machinery (the peel at s;
   `settlement` for the trajectory reading). Tightest build-readiness: S3
   read-back is well-defined because the relation is decidable over finite
   objects; rides law S5. **Mandatory rider (Montague 1963):** `forces` enters
   only as a defined, grounded, decidable relation over finite quoted objects —
   never an axiomatized primitive with reflection schemas.
2. **`(superseded ⌜M⌝ reason)`** — homegrown, "the more organic" (it arises
   from the revision workflow, not an imported dictionary). Discharges the
   **third tense** the validity discipline exposed: *present without force* —
   a withdrawn M held before the mind as an exhibit (first order cannot do
   this; a double cut around old-M still binds). Predicative by construction
   (the quoted state sits strictly below), S1's always-legal case.

**The record's stated leaning.** "**Ship A now; hold B for a demonstrated
need.**" B's whole marginal value is S3 on an *asserted* claim; until the
corpus wants to assert-and-draw-read-back a graph-about-a-graph, the core
touch is unamortised. When B is taken: jointly with the mention fork, B-min
first. Between nominees the record does not rule: `(superseded ⌜M⌝ …)` has
the philosophical edge (organic, already needed by the revision workflow);
`(forces s φ)` has the engineering edge (semantics fully defined today).
Both are hypostatic abstraction applied to a graph; the categories recur one
level up either way (quotations = the new monads, `same_graph` = the new
Secondness, metalearning's principles = informal Thirdness already inhabited —
B decides only whether it gets ink).

**Verdict form (B).** The author affirms / amends:

> **B1.** Ship **Option A now** (the overlay + S1/S2/S4/S5 attestation, S3
> honestly skip-named) — the frontier prepared on `main` after the Moses cut.
> **B2.** The core opens (**B-min**) only when a *demonstrated need* exists: a
> corpus UoD that must **assert** a second-order claim whose read-back matters.
> Name the trigger now: [the author's criterion].
> **B3.** When B-min is taken, it is taken **jointly** with the reference-node
> increment-2 use/mention fork — one core opening, both riders.
> **B4.** The first asserted claim is **[ `(superseded ⌜M⌝ reason)` — organic |
> `(forces s φ)` — exact | both, superseded first ]**, under the Montague rider
> for any `forces`-family relation.
> **B5.** B-full waits for its own demonstrated need after B-min.
