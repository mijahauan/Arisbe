# The Larger Game and the Common Sheet

*Two perspectives — **A** ("the larger game," the discipline applied to *ends*) and
**B** ("the common sheet," the discipline applied to *progress*) — drafted boldly,
then **examined adversarially over four rounds** (one opening panel + an iterative
dissolution-press; the full record, ruling tokens, and confidence are in
[ADVERSARIAL_EXAMINATION.md](ADVERSARIAL_EXAMINATION.md), "Examination II"). They
did **not** survive as independent perspectives. Under pressing they **dissolved
into the three departures plus a conceded structural realism** — the outcome the
author courts: positions lost to good arguments rather than held on conviction.
This document records what they became. The honest shape is **one discipline
(Departures I + II) + one imported equal-dignity premise + a conceded structural
realism, deployed against two targets** — a *corollary* of
[FIDELITY_AND_DEPARTURES.md](FIDELITY_AND_DEPARTURES.md), not a fourth and fifth
departure.*

---

## 0. Why this document exists, and what the examination found

Two perspectives pressed on the doctrine from outside its formal core, each costing
the accomplished reasoner something her accomplishment seems to earn: the referee's
chair (A) and the high rung (B). They were stated, refined by the author in his own
voice, and then handed to opponents the refinement had not faced — a
Peircean-on-the-categories, a structural realist, an agapist, and a dissolution
panel. The verdicts:

- **A is `departure_absorbed`** (confidence ≈0.81). It carries no proposition
  independent of Departure I (negative orientation; the first-order non-locution)
  and Departure II (the low-warrant assertoric posit). It survives only as a small
  *disciplinary office* and as pedagogy.
- **B is `departure_narrowed`** (≈0.75), nearly absorbed. Its propositions absorb
  into Departure I (the dissolved metric terminus) and a *conceded structural
  realism* (the mind-independent comparative efficacy-vector). One genuine residue
  remains: the worth-ladder denial, held at parity in the axiological register.

Neither is the "survives with amendment" the first round reported, and neither is
the "strengthening by subsumption" the author's refinement claimed. The refinement
was the move that fell: A and B are **not** "the level-0 enclosure discipline
applied to ends and progress." That was a category mistake, and it backfired (below).
What follows is the resting place, stated without softening.

## 1. A — the larger game (absorbed into Departures I + II)

A began from a real limit: **there is no surveyable outside to our own reasoning —
no referee's chair we occupy** (the membrane regress, [MANIFEST_AND_MEANING.md](MANIFEST_AND_MEANING.md),
read from the agent's side). "Principalities and powers" was invoked as the **Flying
Spaghetti Monster** is — not to promote His Noodly Appendage but to *demote* every
context-less ultimate end by putting it on the same footing, tradition's respectable
ones (the *terminus ad quem*, the Final Opinion, the Omega) included.

The examination granted the limit and the FSM's deflationary point, and then showed
that **nothing propositional was left over once A's over-reach was removed**:

- The claim that a context-less end is *malformed* "just like the unenclosed
  proposition at level 0" is a **category mistake**. Level-0 unsayability is "a
  syntactic theorem about **marks**" (Secondness); an *end* / final cause is
  **Thirdness** — a real general that governs without being an inscription needing an
  enclosing cut. The marks-theorem does not transfer to ends.
- Worse, run with Departure II's *real* two-register content, the analogy
  **backfires**: Departure II does not dissolve the unenclosed contingent thing — it
  **licenses** it in the assertoric register. So a context-free ultimate end is a
  **legitimate low-warrant assertoric posit, fully sayable, not malformed** (admitted
  at import, exposed to the Agon, never *derived*). A had borrowed the "bare
  cannot-say" slogan the examination of Departure I had already condemned.
- Stripped of that, A's two refusals *are* Departure I: "inquiry **derives** no
  context-free end" is the **negative orientation**; "the game is not scored against
  a surveyable terminus" is the **first-order non-locution**. And the
  posited-vs-served distinction is unstable — a low-warrant ultimate posit that
  *survives the Agon* sits at `withstood ⚔` and is then a served terminus on the only
  reading the gradient permits; A, adding no new restraint, cannot forbid it. Only
  Departure I's non-locution does.

**What survives as A's own:** a small **disciplinary office — the no-founder-
exemption.** The first-order non-locution is universally quantified ("*no* mark
bears its distance-to-the-real as a terminus"), so it already ranges over Omega and
the Final Opinion; what A *performs* is the refusal to grant tradition's respectable,
*on-record* termini the dignity-exemption the owners might have spared them. That is
a use of owned content — a scope of enforcement, and good pedagogy (the FSM is the
memorable way to teach the universal) — not a new proposition. **A is recorded as the
agent-side gloss on Departures I + II, not as a standalone perspective.** Ends in
context — arguments end, innings end, a player conceives a goal and lays plans to win
— are fully real throughout; A obviates no ends, only the context-less terminus *the
game is said to serve*.

## 2. B — the common sheet (narrowed to one axiological residue + a design debt)

B removed the **terminus** and the **worth-ranking** from the convergent dreams
(Peirce's final opinion, Teilhard's Omega) while affirming in-context progress. The
examination split its master-claim ("no progress outside any context") into parts
with opposite fates:

- The context-free **metric terminus** — a possessed summit, a distance-to-the-real
  the gradient scores nearness to — is a **non-locution, dissolved** (Departure I's
  first-order result; "ordinal, a staircase with no top" defends exactly this and
  only this).
- The context-free **comparative efficacy-vector** is **conceded**, not dissolved.
  An instrument is scored by work on a world that does not read our scoreboard —
  novel prediction, intervention that succeeds, the bridge that holds, the augurs'
  eclipse-failures recurring direction-stably across every context. That a later
  practice is "a genuinely better instrument" is therefore a **mind-independent,
  ordinal, directional fact** — and an order with no greatest element (ω) is the
  textbook case of an objective comparative *without* a summit. This is precisely
  **structural realism's** thesis (the *vector*, never the summit), and it sits on
  the **survivor side** — the encountered real cognizable in its effects
  ([FIDELITY_AND_DEPARTURES.md](FIDELITY_AND_DEPARTURES.md) §2). B conceded it the
  moment it said "better instrument," and must not launder it into Departure I's
  honest-parity meta-joint: the efficacy joint is first-order and comparative, and
  the relatum is already conceded.

So B's propositions absorb: metric terminus = Departure I; efficacy-vector = conceded
structural realism; competence ≠ worth = a category-fact every interlocutor grants.

**What survives as B's own:** the **worth-ladder denial aimed at an asserting
opponent.** The convergent dreams *fuse* competence with worth — later inquiry is
nearer the truth, later mind is holier. Severing that fusion — *the real, conceded,
context-transcending competence-vector confers no ranking of the worth, dignity, or
nearness of persons, ages, or cultures* — is content, because the opponent is on
record. *A later stage is a better instrument; its inhabitants are not better souls.*
The child, the prior age, the "savage," the layperson stand at no greater distance in
**worth**, however much better an instrument a later practice wields. Three honest
qualifications the examination forced:

1. It is held **at parity in the axiological register, never as a logical theorem.**
   The moment it is made an entailment of the epistemic non-locution ("nearer the love
   of God"), it overreaches into theology; that clause is **quarantined** as an
   undischarged posit asserted by neither reading, or struck.
2. It rests on an **imported equal-dignity premise** the departures do not derive
   (they are silent on persons). The corpus is free to adopt it, but must own it
   openly, not smuggle it as following from the enclosure discipline.
3. It does **not** flatten cognitive or moral progress: experiment over augury,
   conservation over the preoperational error, abolition over chattel slavery are
   genuine ordinal progress, affirmed — they are advances in *instrument*, not in the
   *worth of souls*.

**The immanent tendency (a correction the examination forced).** B set "context-free
progress" against the wrong thing. A real **immanent operative tendency** — synechism's
continuity, habit-taking, the growth of concrete reasonableness — is operative across
contexts *without* being a terminus one stands outside and scores; the FSM solvent,
scoped to surveyable termini, never touches it. Indeed the corpus already **instances**
the category: the §3.3 correspondence invariant and the secured negative orientation
*are* exercised, immanent, context-transcending corrective tendencies. The cap on this
is **not a scope-cap** ("operative up to Arisbe's contexts, stopping at the cosmos" —
that would be either the referee's chair A forbids or a synechist over-concession). It
is the **enclosure cap**, and it is *won, not wagered*: the tendency is sayable and
operative *wherever a context encloses it*, and **enclosure-malformed only when
scribed as the operative structure of the unenclosable whole** (the outermost sep
cannot be drawn). Agapism thereby wins its **category** (the immanent tendency is real
and instanced); it wins **nothing** of the cosmic verdict, which — as a
*convergence-to-a-terminus* question — remains Departure I's meta-joint, at parity.

## 3. The honest billing, and what the decentering becomes

Counted honestly, there are not two new perspectives. There is **one discipline
(Departures I + II) + one imported equal-dignity premise + a conceded structural
realism**, aimed at two targets: tradition's respectable termini (A) and the
worth-misreading of progress (B). The "two perspectives, one move twice" framing
over-bills; this document is a **corollary of the departures**, and reads best as an
appendix to [FIDELITY_AND_DEPARTURES.md](FIDELITY_AND_DEPARTURES.md).

The **double decentering** survives, but precisely. **A:** you are not the referee —
because there is no surveyable outside (an epistemic limit), not because an agency
runs you (imagery, not an existence claim). **B:** you are not nearer a possessed real
and you are not worth more — though you, or your age, may wield a genuinely better
*instrument*, which is a real, mind-independent, terminus-free fact. What is removed
from the sophisticate is the referee's chair and the conflation of *better instrument*
with *nearer the real* and *worth more*. What is untouched is her competence — and
the activity itself: the playing, in continuity and equal dignity with every other
player, under a push from behind toward no terminus we can score.

## 4. The live floor — the design debt that does not rest

The doctrine rests; the **vigilance it mandates does not**, and is meant never to.
This is the practical residue, booked as a permanent obligation, not an open question:

> At every ranking surface Arisbe builds — the **warrant gradient** in Organon
> (`posited ◇ ‹ derived ⛓ ‹ withstood ⚔`; `provenance.standing_of`), the personas,
> any badge — the ordering must be read as **in-context standing and competence**
> (real, including the conceded context-transcending efficacy-vector), and **never**
> as the **worth or dignity of the reasoner**, **metric nearness** to a possessed
> real, or **context-free Progress**. A badge read as a worth-ladder *is* the
> field-guide dragon (6), whatever the tooltip says — even though the same badge read
> as competence is legitimate.

And A's floor-#6 guard is the same kind of live floor: the agency-reading of
"principalities and powers," taken as a positive existence claim, would bear actuality
across the membrane and is *better not drawn*; it is kept strictly as imagery.

---

## 5. What is won, absorbed, at parity, owed — plainly

- **Won.** The surveyable / served / scored **terminus** is dissolved (the first-order
  non-locution). The **comparative efficacy-vector** and the **in-context immanent
  tendency** are real and sayable (survivor side). The **enclosure cap** on the cosmic
  structure-of-the-whole is won, not wagered.
- **Absorbed.** A → Departures I + II (residue: the no-founder-exemption office,
  kept as pedagogy). B's propositions → Departure I + conceded structural realism.
- **At parity (unchanged, pre-existing).** Departure I's cosmic *convergence-to-a-
  terminus* meta-joint. A and B add nothing to it; they ride it and mark it idle for
  play.
- **Owed.** The **imported equal-dignity premise** (B's surviving residue rests on it;
  own it, don't derive it). The perpetual **design debt** of §4. And A's honest
  admission that its agency-imagery, taken as a claim, is unfalsifiable and would
  breach floor #6 — kept as imagery, never asserted.
