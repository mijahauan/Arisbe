P2 · 3-D nested shells — negation depth on z; fly inward through the cuts (the endoporeutic peel). Drag to orbit, scroll to dolly in.

Each shell = an area; deeper z = deeper negation. Warm/light = recto (asserted); cool/dark = verso (denied). Cream spheres = relations, black = lines of identity. A ligature line literally pierces the shells it must cross (green→red = more crossings).
